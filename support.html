A little help

Here when you need a hand.

Policy details pending. These pages describe the current implementation. Operator and private-support details are still being finalized.
How do I start?

Sign in with your username and password or a configured Google account, review the AI disclosure, and keep the microphone near you. Tap Record to start a session. Mirra records in the background while you focus on your conversation. Tap Stop when you’re done and keep Mirra open while it uploads. Mirra handles the analysis; read your short debrief when ready. Reflect chat and progress views are optional.

How do I personalize suggestions?

Tap Your focus on the recording screen or Profile → Your conversation goal. Choose making friends, confidence, listening, clarity, or speaking up for yourself; Everyday connection leaves the focus open. Your saved choice applies when a new recording is processed and when you use Reflect, including queued recordings processed later. Older debriefs keep their original goal. Changes save automatically; wait for Saving to finish. If a change fails, Mirra shows an error and reloads your saved choice.

How do I sign in?

Use the username and password you registered with, or continue with Google when available. Password recovery for username-only accounts is not currently available.

What happens without internet?

In the mobile app, after signing in and accepting processing, stopped recordings can be saved on your device. Wait until saving finishes before closing the app. The browser version needs an internet connection to load. Keep the app open after reconnecting to upload. The app cannot upload while force-quit. Avoid clearing app or browser data until your recordings have uploaded.

Why is a recording waiting?

Check connectivity, sign-in status, and your monthly usage. Oversized or unsupported recordings stay available to discard. Recordings and imports must fit within 25 MB and be no longer than 60 minutes. The app tells you when processing failed; it should not substitute an invented debrief.

Do the charts know which speaker is me?

No verified identity is available. Mirra estimates that the loudest speaker is you. Microphone placement, overlapping voices, noise and transcription mistakes can change results. Treat the debrief as a prompt for reflection, not a verdict.

How do I delete my data?

Open a conversation and tap Delete. For everything, open Profile → Account actions → Delete account. See web deletion availability and requirements for accounts with an accessible email address. Username-only accounts use the in-app deletion control. Export first if you want a copy.

Can I stop AI processing?

Open Profile → Voice & privacy and withdraw AI consent. This pauses future recording uploads and Reflect requests on that device. You can still export or delete saved data.
