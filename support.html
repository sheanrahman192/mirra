A little help

Here when you need a hand.

Prelaunch preview. Mirra is not yet available in the stores. These policies describe the current implementation; operator and private-support details must be finalized before public release.
How do I start?

Sign in with your username and password or a configured Google account, review the AI disclosure, then ask everyone involved for permission. Keep the microphone near you. Tap Record, have your conversation, then tap Stop and keep Mirra open while it uploads.

How do I sign in?

Use the username and password you registered with, or continue with Google when available. Account recovery for username-only accounts must be finalized before public release.

What happens without internet?

After you have signed in and accepted processing, stopped recordings can wait safely on your device. Keep the app open after reconnecting to upload. The app cannot upload while force-quit. Avoid clearing app or browser data until your recordings have uploaded.

Why is a recording waiting?

Check connectivity, sign-in status, and your monthly usage. Oversized or unsupported recordings stay available to discard. Imports must be under 25 MB and no longer than 60 minutes. The app tells you when processing failed; it should not substitute an invented debrief.

Do the charts know which speaker is me?

No verified identity is available. Mirra estimates that the loudest speaker is you. Microphone placement, overlapping voices, noise and transcription mistakes can change results. Treat the debrief as a prompt for reflection, not a verdict.

How do I delete my data?

Open a conversation and tap Delete. For everything, open Profile → Account actions → Delete account. You can also use account deletion on the web. Export first if you want a copy.

Can I stop AI processing?

Open Profile → Voice & privacy and withdraw AI consent. This pauses future recording uploads and Reflect requests on that device. You can still export or delete saved data.

Contact

Private support contact is being finalized before launch. Do not post recordings, transcripts, passwords, or personal information on public issue trackers.
