A small practice

Notice. Reflect.
Try again.

No score to chase. Just a clearer view of your habits and a practical next step.

01 / Make space

Ask, then record.

Get everyone's permission before recording or importing audio. Stop and save when you're done; an offline recording waits on your device for a connection.

02 / Look back

Find the pattern.

Review estimated speaking time, questions, interruptions, and other signals alongside a short AI debrief.

03 / Move forward

Take one small step.

Explore a moment in Reflect, choose something to practice, and return to your conversations over time.

Read how your data is handled ↗

Mirra uses OpenAI for transcription and coaching, and Supabase for your account and saved debriefs. You choose whether to save transcripts.

  • Explicit permission before AI processing.
  • Delete a conversation or your whole account.
  • Export your saved data.
  • No ads or advertising trackers in the app.

AI and speaker estimates can be wrong. Mirra is a reflection tool, not medical or mental-health care.
