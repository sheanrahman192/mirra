Using Mirra responsibly

Terms of Use

Version September 15, 2026

Policy details pending. These pages describe the current implementation. Operator and private-support details are still being finalized.

The service

Mirra helps adults reflect on their conversational habits using recorded audio, estimated statistics, and AI-generated coaching. Mirra offers up to five debriefs per account per calendar month and up to 60 Reflect messages per hour. Deleting a completed debrief does not restore usage. Processing, availability, and results may vary.

Permission to record and process

You must be 18 or older and have permission from everyone involved before recording or uploading a conversation for Mirra and OpenAI to process. Comply with the laws and rules that apply to the participants and location, including workplace and professional confidentiality. Do not secretly record people, impersonate them, upload unlawful content, or use Mirra for surveillance or harassment. Stop recording if permission is withdrawn.

Your content and account

You retain rights in your content. You give the service only the permission needed to process it, provide your debriefs, and operate the features you choose. Keep your sign-in credentials private and use an account you control. Do not attempt to access another person's data, circumvent usage limits, or interfere with the service. You may export your saved data or delete your account at any time through the available controls.

Understand the limits

AI can invent details or misunderstand context. Speaker identification assumes the loudest speaker is the account holder and may be wrong. Acoustic and language measurements are estimates, not verified assessments of personality, emotions, health, relationships, honesty, or competence. Do not use them to make employment, educational, medical, legal or other consequential decisions about someone.

Mirra is not a clinician, therapist, diagnostic service, legal adviser, emergency service or substitute for professional care. It does not monitor conversations for emergencies. Seek appropriate professional or emergency help when needed.

Availability and changes

We cannot guarantee uninterrupted service or a particular outcome. Offline recordings upload only when the app is open with a working connection. Stopping and saving a recording before closing the app is necessary for recovery. Device loss, uninstalling, or clearing browser data may erase local recordings. Important changes to the service or these terms will be communicated through the app or published notices.

Private support contact is being finalized. Do not post recordings, transcripts, passwords, or personal information on public issue trackers.
