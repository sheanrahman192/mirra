Delete your account.

This permanently removes your Mirra account, saved debriefs, transcripts, settings and monthly usage records. It cannot be undone. Export a copy in the app first if you want one. Locally queued recordings on other devices should be discarded there or removed by clearing the app's data.

The app also supports deletion from Profile → Account actions → Delete account.
