Privacy Policy

Version September 14, 2026

Prelaunch preview. Mirra is not yet available in the stores. These policies describe the current implementation; operator and private-support details must be finalized before public release.

Who is responsible

The service operator is not yet confirmed for public launch. Contact: Private support contact is being finalized before launch. Do not post recordings, transcripts, passwords, or personal information on public issue trackers.

What the app processes

DataPurpose and storage
Account detailsEmail, account ID, authentication credentials or provider identifiers, and preferences are managed by Supabase Auth and our database.
RecordingsYour device saves stopped recordings until a successful upload or explicit discard. Our backend temporarily decodes the audio and sends it to OpenAI for transcription; we do not keep an audio archive on our backend.
Transcripts and debriefsOpenAI receives the labeled conversation and derived statistics for coaching. Debriefs and statistics are saved to your account. Transcript storage is off for new users unless you enable it. Existing saved transcripts are not erased simply by switching this preference off.
Reflect messagesMessages, recent chat context, and relevant debriefs go to OpenAI. Saved transcript excerpts are included only when you enable that setting. Reflect chat history stays in the current app session and is not saved by Mirra as a separate chat archive.
Operational recordsHosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps and security logs. Monthly usage counters enforce the debrief limit. We do not intentionally log audio, transcript text or chat prompts.

AI and service providers

OpenAI provides transcription and AI coaching. Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Requests for text coaching use store=false; this does not eliminate all provider retention. OpenAI's API data controls describe endpoint-specific handling and possible abuse-monitoring retention, generally up to 30 days for text requests unless an exception applies. API content is not used to train OpenAI models by default. See OpenAI's data controls and Supabase's privacy policy.

Permission and choices

You explicitly agree before AI processing. Record only after everyone involved permits both recording and processing. Recording rules depend on location and circumstances; permission from you alone may not be sufficient. Do not upload sensitive conversations you do not have permission to share.

You can withdraw AI consent from Profile. This stops future processing; it cannot recall a request already sent. Saved conversations remain available for viewing, export, or deletion. Microphone access can be revoked in device settings. The app does not use location, contact lists, advertising IDs, advertising trackers, or health-record integrations.

Retention and deletion

Saved debriefs and optional transcripts remain until you delete them or your account. A content-free deletion marker remains until account deletion to prevent delayed uploads from restoring a deleted conversation. Delete a conversation from its detail screen. Delete your account from Profile → Account actions → Delete account. This removes the Supabase account and associated debriefs, usage and preferences; local queued recordings on that device are also removed. Other devices may retain offline files until their app data is removed; uninstall Mirra or clear its local storage on devices you no longer use.

Backups and providers' security or abuse-monitoring records may persist under their configured retention and legal obligations. Deletion from the live database does not promise immediate removal from every backup. The production backup schedule and hosting retention must be recorded before launch.

Your rights and safety

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal data and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Use the in-app export and deletion controls or web deletion page. Contact information and any applicable regional representative must be finalized before public release. Mirra is intended for adults 18 and over and is not designed for children.

Data may be processed outside your country by the named providers. Production regions, applicable transfer safeguards, and the operator's legal basis for processing must be reviewed before expanding release territories. We use authenticated requests and account-scoped access controls, but no system can promise absolute security.

Changes

Material changes to AI processing require a new consent version. The current version is displayed above.
